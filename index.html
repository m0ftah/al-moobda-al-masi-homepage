About Us

A Libyan company built on trusted import relationships

Al-Moobda Al-Masi Company for Importing Industrial and Marine Equipment is a Libyan company dedicated to sourcing and supplying the equipment that keeps industrial and marine operations running across the country.

We supply major oil and gas companies in Libya with all the machinery, tools, and equipment needed to ensure their operations stay maintained — sourced, imported, and delivered by a team that understands what the field requires.

Our work isn't limited to the oil and gas sector — we also operate and hold contracts with The Great Man-Made River Project in Libya.

We work directly with Libyan companies and operators, managing the full import process — sourcing, procurement, and delivery — so our clients get the equipment they need without the complexity of dealing with overseas suppliers themselves.

For over ten years, we have built and maintained registrations and working relationships with many of Libya's major companies, giving clients confidence in our reliability and our standing with local authorities and operators.
